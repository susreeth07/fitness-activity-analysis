# Activity Visualization Summary

## 1. Steps Per Day

The steps-per-day visualization shows the daily activity level using total steps recorded for each activity record. It helps identify variations in daily physical activity.

## 2. Calories Burned

The calories-burned visualization shows calories burned across the recorded activity dates. It helps visualize changes in daily energy expenditure.

## 3. Sleep Duration vs Activity

The sleep-duration-vs-activity visualization compares total minutes asleep with total steps for matched participant-day records. It helps examine the relationship between sleep duration and physical activity.
